# Privacy Policy — Kuller (Estonian for couriers)

_Last updated: 2026-06-19_

**Kuller** is an offline language‑learning game. We respect your privacy.

## Data we collect
**None.** The app does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal data.
It has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network
requests.

## Data stored on your device
The app saves only your **learning progress** (money, XP, rating, learned words,
and the “hide translations” preference) locally on your device using Android
`SharedPreferences`. This data never leaves your phone and is removed when you
press **“Сбросить прогресс”** (Reset progress) in the Profile screen or when you
uninstall the app.

## Permissions
The app requests **no special permissions**. It uses the device’s built‑in
**Text‑to‑Speech** engine to pronounce Estonian words; the spoken text is
processed by your system TTS and is not sent anywhere by us.

## Children
The app contains no objectionable content and collects no data, so it is safe
for all ages.

## Third parties
None. The app bundles only open‑source Android (Jetpack Compose) libraries.
